# Standalone demo sources

The 45-second, 1920×1080, 30fps demo uses real isolated visual previews from
Hello Lock at e1dd95d and the actual standalone Studio capture from the final
Arch VM run. The opening and unlock payoff are visual previews, not recordings
of authentication. Native PAM verification is documented separately in the
release review. The source visual components and final tested standalone
runtime are identified in the release records.

Omarchy branding and Tokyo Night styling lead the film. NOVA is sample branding
generated with the bundled wordmark helper; the colored Nova insignia is a
bundled sample image. No private MARY collection, owner desktop, microphone or
desktop audio is included. Oxanium and Share Tech Mono retain their bundled OFL
notices. Sound cues come from Hello Lock's own sound pack. HyperFrames renders
the composition; its cardless lower-third component informed the title layout.
No stock footage, stock music or generated voice is used.

Final export: H.264 video and stereo AAC, 45.000 seconds. Frame sheets, cut
boundaries and decoded frames were inspected. Audio measured -16.8 LUFS and
-1.5 dBTP with no clipping; it was measured, not heard. The video demonstrates
customization, not authentication performance or uninterrupted lock protection.
